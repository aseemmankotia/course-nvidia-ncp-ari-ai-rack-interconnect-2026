## Chapter 3: Site Assessment Before Deployment — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | Typical concentrated load rating of a standard data center raised floor? | 150-250 lbs/sq ft (732-1,220 kg/m²). Exam tip: high-density AI racks often exceed this per-tile, requiring structural engineer sign-off before install. |
| 2 | NEC 110.26(A)(1) minimum working clearance in front of electrical equipment (Condition 1, 0-150V to ground)? | 36 inches (914 mm). Exam tip: this is a life-safety code requirement, not a suggestion — flag any site survey measurement below this. |
| 3 | Typical minimum rear clearance for high-density racks (airflow + service access)? | 30 inches (762 mm). Exam tip: distinct from the 36-inch NEC front working clearance — don't mix these numbers up. |
| 4 | ASHRAE Class A1 RECOMMENDED intake temperature range? | 18-27°C (64-80°F). Exam tip: 'recommended' range is narrower than the wider 'allowable' range (up to ~32°C) — exam questions test which range a scenario violates. |
| 5 | Approximate weight of an NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 rack? | ~1,362 kg (3,000 lbs). Exam tip: this weight, divided by the rack's ~0.72 m² footprint, typically exceeds standard raised floor ratings — always calculate lbs/sq ft, not total weight alone. |
| 6 | Site survey vs. Site assessment — key distinction? | Survey = physical on-site measurement/observation. Assessment = broader evaluation combining survey data + design docs + readiness criteria for go/no-go decision. Exam tip: assessment is the output; survey is the input. |
| 7 | Who determines whether a raised floor can support a new high-density rack row? | A structural engineer, using the building's certified load rating — not visual inspection or comparison to existing racks. Exam tip: technicians escalate; they don't self-certify structural capacity. |
| 8 | Static load vs. dynamic (rolling) load in floor capacity planning — why does it matter? | Static load = rack sitting in place; dynamic/rolling load = weight while being wheeled in on casters, often requiring a HIGHER momentary rating along the delivery path. Exam tip: verify the rolling path separately from the final resting position. |
| 9 | What must be verified before connecting a liquid-cooled rack to a facility CDU? | Supply water temperature and flow rate must meet the rack's specified operating range. Exam tip: this is an environmental readiness gate — install cannot proceed to power-on without it, regardless of physical placement being complete. |
| 10 | What should physical network design documentation be validated against during a site survey? | Actual as-built site conditions — cable pathway routing, power drop locations, and rack placement. Exam tip: design docs can be outdated; the survey confirms reality matches the design before install. |
| 11 | What is included in a pre-install inventory-check procedure? | Verify all shipped components (racks, PDUs, cable kits, rail kits, CDUs, spares) against the packing manifest and site readiness checklist before opening install tickets. Exam tip: missing components discovered mid-install cause costly schedule delays — check first. |
| 12 | What does a 'pre-install documentation package' typically include? | Physical network design drawings, floor load assessment, environmental readiness report, cable pathway plan, and inventory manifest. Exam tip: exam scenarios test which single missing document blocks a valid go/no-go decision. |
