# Chapter 3 Lab: Site Assessment Before Deployment

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Complete a site-readiness checklist against a sample floor plan, verifying floor load rating (lbs/sq ft), rack spacing, and thermal zone alignment for a planned GB200 NVL72 deployment

## Key takeaway

Site assessment must be completed and documented before any physical install work begins, catching structural or environmental gaps early.
