**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Review a labeled GB200 NVL72 rack cross-section diagram and map each physical layer (power shelf, compute tray, NVSwitch tray, CDU manifold) to its corresponding interconnect type

Correctly mapping the GB200 NVL72's power, cooling, and interconnect layers to their respective technologies is foundational to nearly every other exam domain.
