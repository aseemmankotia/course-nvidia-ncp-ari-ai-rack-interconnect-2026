# Chapter 4 Lab: Preparing the Rack for High-Density Install

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Using a rack elevation diagram, position vertical cable-management ducts and verify front-post-to-door and inter-rack clearances against NVIDIA DU-10438 spec values

## Key takeaway

Rack preparation clearances and EMI separation must follow platform-specific specs, not generic rack assumptions.
