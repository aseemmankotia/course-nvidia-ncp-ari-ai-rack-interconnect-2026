# Chapter 7 Lab: Routing, Bend Radius, and Professional Dressing

**Estimated time:** 25 minutes

## Exercise

Using a blank NVL72 rear-rack cabling diagram, apply the 50/50 left-right routing strategy, mark bend-radius checkpoints, and assign color codes per fabric type

## Key takeaway

Routing strategy and bend-radius compliance directly determine both signal integrity and pass/fail inspection outcomes.
