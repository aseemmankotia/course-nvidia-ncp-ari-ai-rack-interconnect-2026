# Chapter 11 Lab: Safety and Compliance in High-Power Environments

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Review a sample installation scenario and select the correct cable flame rating (CMP vs CMR) and PPE/LOTO steps required before working on an energized 120kW rack row

## Key takeaway

Safety and code compliance, including NVIDIA-specific DU-10438 requirements, are treated as mandatory verification steps, not optional best practices.
