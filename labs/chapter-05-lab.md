# Chapter 5 Lab: Cable and Transceiver Selection

**Estimated time:** 25 minutes

## Exercise

Given a spec sheet of six cable/transceiver samples, sort them by NVIDIA-certified vs third-party, OSFP vs QSFP, and DAC vs ACC classification

## Key takeaway

Cable and transceiver selection is a distance-and-data-rate decision, not a one-size-fits-all default.
