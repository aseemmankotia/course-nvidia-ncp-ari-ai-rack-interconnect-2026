# Chapter 10 Lab: Final Inspection, Documentation, and BER Troubleshooting

**Estimated time:** 25 minutes

## Exercise

Given a troubleshooting scenario with a high-BER link, walk through the decision tree (check connector cleanliness, then bend radius, then transceiver seating, then escalate) and produce a documented as-built note

## Key takeaway

Systematic BER troubleshooting and complete as-built documentation are what separate a passing install from an unverifiable one.
