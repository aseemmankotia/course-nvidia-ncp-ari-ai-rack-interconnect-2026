# Chapter 1 Lab: AI Rack Power Fundamentals

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Using a public-style NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD power design reference, label a single-line power diagram for a GB200 NVL72 rack, identifying rPDU phases A/B/C, redundant feeds, and breaker points

## Key takeaway

Power redundancy and rPDU phase balancing must be verified manually against rack-specific load calculations, not assumed from breaker labels alone.
