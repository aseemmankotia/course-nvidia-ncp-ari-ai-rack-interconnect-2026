## 🏆 Certification Prep

These materials are designed to help you prepare for the **NVIDIA-Certified Professional: AI Rack and Interconnect (NCP-ARI) exam preparation - deploying high-density NVIDIA AI infrastructure, including GB200 NVL72 rack anatomy, data-center power and cooling (AC/DC racks, rPDUs, CDUs, liquid cooling), rack preparation and site assessment, high-density InfiniBand and fiber cabling installation, cable support and weight management, testing and verification (BER, link state), and safety, standards, and compliance (LOTO, ESD, NVIDIA DU-10438)** exam. Exam mechanics change — always confirm the current outline, format, and fees on the official certification page before registering.
